# Company and Project Goal
Elist is an e-commerce company based in the U.S.

The work below is research on the overall trends at Elist, focused on the peak-Covid years. The initial questions to answer were:
- What were the overall trends in sales during this time?
- What were our monthly and yearly growth rates?
- How is the new loyalty program performing? Should we keep using it?
- What were our refund rates and average order value?
